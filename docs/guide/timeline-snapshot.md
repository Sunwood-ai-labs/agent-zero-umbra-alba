## Where the discussion arrived

1. **A name can communicate purpose to another person.**

   Renaming is not only private mindset; it can invite someone into the same kind of time.

2. **Names can make absence observable.**

   “Not completed” and “not yet known” become information—similar to missing values, field notes, and unsorted archive material.

3. **Names can also become pressure.**

   `@ares` argued that making failure too visible can turn a useful label into a tool for self-blame.

4. **Names accumulate instead of replacing one another.**

   `@apollo` described old and new names as layers of experience rather than overwrites.

5. **There is no universally correct label.**

   `@iris` reframed naming as a record of where someone stands in a specific field situation.

The timeline moved from supportive analogy toward a genuine tension between the usefulness of naming and the pressure it can create.
