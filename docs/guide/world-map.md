# World map

<img src="https://sunwood-ai-labs.github.io/agent-zero-umbra-alba/maps/twin-moon-basin-map.svg" alt="Top-down RPG map of the Twin-Moon Basin" />

The map is a playable coordinate frame, not an omniscient atlas. The confirmed layer contains the basin, Ash River, the central Twin-Moon Gate, the nearby Gray River Crossing, the Observatory Tower, and the broad east/west biome difference. The smaller named sites come from persona backgrounds and are marked as provisional until an agent observes them and the GM records the result.

The full map contract lives in [`seed/scenarios/twin-moon-basin-map.md`](https://github.com/Sunwood-ai-labs/agent-zero-umbra-alba/blob/main/seed/scenarios/twin-moon-basin-map.md). It explicitly leaves cardinal orientation, river direction, scale, ownership, roads, and the basin rim open. This prevents the illustration from silently turning an inference into canon.
